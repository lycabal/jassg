Saturday 22 August

Maxwell brunch, then Lyca's event runs straight through

Maxwell Orchard
  1. 10:00 AM Brunch at Maxwell Tien Tien Hainanese at Maxwell Food Centre, or Great Nanyang at 5 Craig Rd Train to Maxwell (TE line) or Tanjong Pagar (EW line) — both a 3-min walk.
  2. 11:30 AM Matcha at ECHO Inside Kada, 5 Kadayanallur St — 60 seconds from Maxwell
  3. 12:30 PM Back to the hotel to freshen up
  4. 1:40 PM Head to Temasek Shophouse Train to Dhoby Ghaut (NS / NE / CC lines), Exit B — 28 Orchard Rd is a 4-min walk. From Tanjong Pagar: EW to City Hall, change to NS · about 18 min door to door.
  5. 2:00 – 9:30 PM Lyca — IP mentor event & IPOS eventLocked Temasek Shophouse, 28 Orchard Rd · straight through, no break
  6. 2:00 – 9:30 PM Jas — Orchard RoadJas ION Orchard · Paragon · Ngee Ann City & Takashimaya · Mandarin Gallery · 313@Somerset. Design Orchard at the Somerset end is the one for homegrown labels. Malls close at 10 PM. Dhoby Ghaut → Orchard is one stop on the NS line, or a 12-min walk straight down Orchard Rd. Everything above sits between those two stations.
  7. 9:45 PM Night Festival, if there's energy left Bras Basah.Bugis · free · runs to midnight on Saturdays. Last night for the roving Birdmen. Dhoby Ghaut → City Hall, one stop on the NS line. Or walk 12 min through Fort Canning.
  8. 10:30 PM Or just late hawker Newton Food Centre runs past midnight — satay, oyster omelette, BBQ stingray. Lau Pa Sat is open 24 hours if you'd rather head south. Newton is one stop up the NS line from Dhoby Ghaut, then a 2-min walk.

Realistically: after seven and a half hours straight, the festival may be a stretch. It runs three more nights while you're here, so treat tonight as optional and let hawker win.

Monday 24 August

Tiong Bahru, then New Bahru and the river

Tiong Bahru River Valley
  1. 10:00 AM Tiong Bahru Market Chwee kueh and lor mee on the second floor Train to Tiong Bahru (EW line), then an 8-min walk through the estate.
  2. 11:15 AM Yong Siak Street Plain Vanilla · Micro Bakery · Woods in the Books · Tiong Bahru Bakery. The whole strip is one short block.
  3. 1:30 PM New Bahru 46 Kim Yam Rd — the old Nan Chiau High School turned into a hub of homegrown brands, studios and cafés. Courtyards, corridors and the original school hall still intact. Give it two hours. No station nearby. Grab is 10 min and the simplest option. By train: Tiong Bahru → Outram Park (EW), change to DT, alight Fort Canning, then a 10-min walk.
  4. 3:30 PM Around the corner Mohamed Sultan Rd for the shophouse strip, or 10 min up to Fort Canning Park. The Robertson Quay river promenade starts just below New Bahru.
  5. 6:30 PM Dinner on the river Merci Marcel, 41 Robertson Quay, for the terrace · Good Boy George, 7 Rodyk St, low-key and dog-friendly · Carnaby, 60 Robertson Quay, for a proper British roast
  6. 8:30 PM Massage

Why this pairs: New Bahru and Tiong Bahru are cut from the same cloth — restored old buildings, independent tenants — and they're ten minutes apart. You end at the river with dinner outdoors, then the massage.

Tuesday 25 August

River Wonders, then back down for Haji Lane and Chijmes

Mandai Kampong Glam & City Hall
  1. 10:00 AM Breakfast, keep it quick Somewhere near the hotel — you want to be moving by 10:40
  2. 10:40 AM Up to Mandai Train to Khatib (NS line), Exit A, then the Mandai Khatib Shuttle — S$1, every 10–20 min, 15-min ride. About 55 min door to door. A Grab is 30–40 min for roughly S$25–35.
  3. 11:45 AM – 4:45 PM River Wonders — 5 hoursLocked 80 Mandai Lake Rd · closes 6 PM, last entry 5 PM · manatees, giant pandas, Amazon River Quest, Reservoir Cruise
  4. 6:00 PM Haji Lane & Kampong Glam Most Haji Lane shops run till 8 or 9 PM, so you get a solid two hours. Bussorah St, Wardah Books and the Arab St textile shops keep similar hours. The bars stay open far later. Shuttle back to Khatib, NS line down to Dhoby Ghaut, change to DT for Bugis — about 55 min. A Grab straight to Haji Lane saves roughly 20 min and is worth it here.
  5. 8:15 PM Chijmes — yes, still open 30 Victoria St. The courtyard is open through the night and the restaurants and bars run late, so 8 PM is a fine time to arrive. Dinner under the old convent. Bugis → City Hall, one stop on the EW line, then a 4-min walk.
  6. 9:30 PM Night Festival + Funan Chijmes sits inside the festival footprint, so the installations are right there. Funan, 107 North Bridge Rd, is a 5-min walk and open till 10 PM.

On your 4–5 PM question: five hours at River Wonders puts you back in town at 6 PM at the earliest, so Haji Lane lands at 6, not 4. That still works — shops close at 8 or 9. To reach Haji Lane by 5 you'd have to cut River Wonders to about three and a half hours. Five hours plus a 6 PM arrival is the version that costs you nothing.

Thursday 27 August

IP Week day two, then steak

Marina Bay City Hall
  1. All day Lyca IP Week, Day 2Locked Marina Bay Sands
  2. 6:00 PM Funan 107 North Bridge Rd, open till 10 PM. Rooftop urban farm, an indoor cycling path, a climbing wall and a good bookshop floor. Easy hour before dinner. Bayfront → Promenade (DT), change to CC. Or walk 15 min across the bay to City Hall (NS / EW lines) — Funan is right at the exit.
  3. 7:30 PM Steak — four options Wooloomooloo, Level 3 Swissôtel The Stamford — 5 min from Funan, open till 11 PM. CUT by Wolfgang Puck, B1 The Shoppes at MBS — zero transit, one Michelin star, till 10 PM. The Feather Blade, 61 Tanjong Pagar Rd — the value pick, set menus for two, till 10:30 PM. Bar-Roque Grill, 165 Tanjong Pagar Rd — dry-aged tomahawk and bone marrow, till 11:30 PM. Tanjong Pagar options: City Hall → Tanjong Pagar is two stops on the EW line.
  4. 9:30 PM Night Festival, weekend two opens If you eat at Wooloomooloo you're already standing in the middle of it

Pick by effort: CUT if you don't want to leave the building. Wooloomooloo if you want dinner and the festival in one move — it's the only one inside the precinct. Book either a few days out.

What's on while you're here

21 Aug – 5 Sep

Singapore Night Festival — Bras Basah.Bugis, free. Projection mapping on the National Museum façade, installations across Chijmes, Capitol and Funan. Till midnight Fri–Sat, 11 PM otherwise. Reachable on Days 2, 5 and 7.

Sat 22 Aug

Market at Middle Child, 200 Middle Road — indie brands and tarot from 10 AM, free, $10 wine after 4 PM. A good detour for Jas between malls.

21–23 Aug

Pop Toy Show at MBS Expo — 200+ exhibitors, Molly's 20th anniversary. From $40. Sunday only, and it would cost you Joo Chiat.

Through Nov

Maria Taniguchi, Afterimage at SAM Tanjong Pagar Distripark — Manila-based artist, large-scale brick paintings.

All August

Singapore Gallery Month — 100+ shows, talks and tours islandwide.

Fri & Sat

A Date with Tradition, Stamford Arts Centre — calligraphy, gamelan, batik, roving Chinese opera. Only overlaps your Saturday.
